Results

Six accounts, with the real numbers

These are client accounts Dale managed personally. Figures are as reported by the sellers. Results vary by category, margin, and how much room the account had to begin with.

Magic Brand

$0 → $450K/month

In four months.

A cold launch. No sales history, no reviews, no ranking. Built from zero with manual bids from day one rather than letting Amazon's algorithm learn on the seller's money.

Midtown Umbrellas — Yoan

$6K → $300K/month

In three months.

A seasonal product with a narrow selling window. The account had volume potential it was never structured to capture. Restructure first, then scale bids into the season.

Zestt Organics

$50K → $110K/month

In two months — with a 40% cut in ad spend.

The clearest version of the argument. Revenue more than doubled while spend went down by nearly half. Nothing changed about the products. The bids changed.

Temp Tooth

70% less ad spend

Revenue went $90K → $120K/month at the same time.

This account was badly overpaying per click. Turning off dynamic bidding and multipliers took most of the waste out before any structural work started.

Patriot Crew

$174K → $388K/month

An already-healthy account that had plateaued. The ceiling wasn't demand, it was a campaign structure built for a much smaller catalog.

Emerald Plastics

$6,545/month by month two

From a cold launch.

Units sold up 164%. Smaller numbers than the rest, and included on purpose — this is what an early-stage account actually looks like in its first sixty days.
