Questions

Who is this actually for?
Amazon sellers doing $100K+/month in revenue and spending at least $10K/month on ads. Most of our clients spend $20–30K/month or more. If you're under $10K/month in ad spend, the math doesn't work yet — we'll tell you that on the call instead of taking your money.
Do you take everyone who applies?
No. We audit your account before we take you on. If we look at your data and don't see enough waste to hit the guarantee, we'll say so and you keep your money. We only work with accounts we're confident we can fix.
What's the guarantee?
Cut your ad spend by 25% in the first 30 days without losing sales. If we miss it, you get your money back or we keep working free until we hit it. The guarantee is only offered after the audit, once we've seen the account. Clients commonly go on to see 50% by day 60–90, but 25% in 30 days is the number we guarantee.
How do you cut spend without killing my sales?
Most accounts are bleeding money on Amazon's dynamic bidding and placement multipliers — settings that quietly let Amazon charge more per click than you agreed to. We strip those out and run manual, top-down bids. Same sales, less spend. We're not cutting your budget, we're cutting your waste.
Will this hurt my organic rankings?
No. We manage bids down, we don't blanket-block keywords. Rankings you've already built cost far less to hold than they did to earn.
How is this different from the agency I'm already using?

Three things:

  • You work with Dale directly. No junior account manager, no rotating staff.
  • 24-hour response time, every time.
  • No percentage of ad spend. Most agencies get paid more when you spend more — which is exactly the wrong incentive if you want your spend cut.
How much of my time does this take?
15–30 minutes a month to review the report. Slack access to Dale if you need anything in between.
What happens after I book?
You get a confirmation right away. On the call, Dale walks through your actual account — where the multipliers are, which campaigns are fragmented, what keywords are burning money — and gives you the plan. If you're a fit, we tell you. If you're not, we tell you that too.
