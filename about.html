About Dale

Dale is an inventor and entrepreneur who scaled his own brands to seven figures on Amazon before he ever took on a client.

Since starting the agency on Upwork, he's become the highest-ranked "Amazon Expert" in the world on the platform. He's also the founder and CEO of Brainy Brands.

Unlike most agencies, Dale personally oversees and manages every part of your account. Consistent communication, dedicated attention, and no being passed between account managers. That's the Brainy Brands promise.

Why the model matters more than the bio

Ever wonder why agencies that claim deep Amazon expertise have never run a product of their own? We've private-labelled, innovated products, and built seven-figure brands in our own portfolio. Everything we do on your account is built on something we've already done on ours.

Most Amazon agencies are built to scale headcount. They hire junior managers, give each one fifteen to thirty accounts, and lean on software to make the bid decisions. When someone quits, your account gets reassigned and the new person starts learning your catalog from scratch.

If you've been through that two or three times, you already know the cost. Nothing compounds. Every quarter is a reintroduction.

Brainy Brands is deliberately small so that doesn't happen. Fewer clients, one manager, and a hard cap on how many accounts we take at once. We also only take a client on when we're confident we can deliver real value — if we don't think we can, we'll tell you. If we're full, we'll tell you that too.

Our mission

Concrete, measurable results. Since 2013 we've worked alongside some very successful Amazon sellers, and we've kept learning new techniques the whole way rather than settling into one playbook.

Our aim is a long relationship where you're happy to pay us because the value is obvious — not a short engagement that looks good on our revenue and mediocre on your P&L.
